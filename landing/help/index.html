How it counts

Send us your proof. A computer checks every step. If it passes, it counts. No one has to trust you, and you do not have to trust us.

Send your proof as a pull request. It counts when the automatic check passes: Lean builds it, with no sorry and no shortcut. Nobody has to trust you, and you do not have to trust us.

Open a pull request against lean-proofs. It counts when CI passes: lake build succeeds, the file contains no sorry, and the axiom printout shows nothing beyond the standard three (no native_decide, no new axioms). Trust rests on the Lean kernel, not on you or on us.
