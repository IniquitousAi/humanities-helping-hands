How many real questions does proof settle?

Daniel had a hunch before counting. He thought proof solves 80% of real questions. This page counts real questions. It reports the true number.

Daniel's guess, stated before any counting: something proven to work settles at least 80% of everything, and the rest is guesses and luck. This page counts it on real questions people asked, and reports the number whatever it says.

Empirical evaluation of the 80% provability conjecture across real-world query corpuses, quantifying the exact fraction resolvable via deterministic formal verification versus empirical evidence.

II.How it is counted

Real questions. We picked real questions at random. A computer reads them. It sorts them into simple piles. We count every single one.

Proven answers. When a fact is PROVEN by math, we log it. We check that two tools agree. Every check is open for you to see.

II.How it is counted

The questions. 490 real questions drawn at random (seed 20261029) from 50 of the 151 main Stack Exchange sites, 100 questions before one random moment in each site's life. The first 100 are labeled so far; the rest follow.

The four buckets, written down before the first label: proven to work (proof, logic, computation, or a repeatable procedure known to work), data and sources, a choice of taste or values, and luck.

Two labelers, Sonnet and Haiku, each alone. They agree on 90% of questions (Cohen's kappa 0.837, where 1 is perfect and 0 is chance).

A third labeler from another family, Qwen3.5 9B (made by Alibaba, run on this PC), checks that the agreement is not one company's models agreeing with themselves. All three agree on 65% of questions. Kappa, MEASURED on these 100:

PairKappa
Sonnet vs Haiku0.837
Sonnet vs Qwen0.51
Haiku vs Qwen0.519

Read plainly: the two Claude models agree far more with each other than either does with Qwen, so part of the 0.837 is one family agreeing with itself. The headline numbers above stand as Sonnet's and Haiku's, and this gap is the reason Daniel's hand check matters.

Daniel also checks 20 at random by hand; his matches will be shown here.

II.Methodology and Inter-Rater Reliability

Sampling protocol. Stratified pseudo-random sampling (seed 20261029) across 50 of 151 Stack Exchange sites, drawing 490 items prior to randomized temporal cutoffs; initial cohort N=100.

Taxonomic taxonomy. Four mutually exclusive partitions: PROVEN formal deduction, empirical measurement, subjective normative choices, and stochastic outcomes.

Inter-annotator agreement. Multi-model evaluation across Claude 3.5 Sonnet and Haiku (90% concordance, Cohen kappa 0.837; 1 is perfect agreement, 0 is chance). Daniel validates 20 random samples.
