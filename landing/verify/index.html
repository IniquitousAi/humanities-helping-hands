Repeat every check yourself

You do not have to trust us. Every check below can be done again on your own computer.You never have to take our word for it. Each step below repeats one of our checks on your own machine.Every claim the site makes can be re-derived without us: file hashes, the timestamp, the Lean proofs.

  1. The files. Download manifest.txt: one line per published file, its SHA-256 and its path. Fetch any file (for example /facts.json), run sha256sum on it, and the number must match its line. Data files and scripts match byte for byte. Pages match once Cloudflare's additions are taken out: it adds its analytics script and hides email addresses on the way out.
  2. When. manifest.txt.ots anchors the manifest's fingerprint in Bitcoin. Check it at opentimestamps.org, or run ots verify manifest.txt.ots. More on the stamp.
  3. The proofs. Clone lean-proofs and run ./RERUN.sh (RERUN.cmd on Windows). The first run takes 20 to 40 minutes while Lean fetches Mathlib, then prints PASS or FAIL for each claim. The Lean files are in the manifest too, so you can see they are the ones we stamped. The Root Theorem also re-checks in your browser on the check page.
  4. A stamp. Paste it in the box above. Your browser checks our signature with our public key and finds it in the public log.

The site's own test suite is not public yet, so that check cannot be repeated from outside today.

What is open, and what you pay for

Reading is open to everyone. Using our facts is open to everyone. Paid (not on sale yet): a stamp, an Ed25519 signature over your hash, label and date, appended to the public hash-chained log; and a check, a machine check we run on your input. Labels come only from the checkers; money never touches them.

The stamp is math. Check it yourself. If the math says real, it's real. Still think something is wrong after a VALID check? Send us the evidence.
