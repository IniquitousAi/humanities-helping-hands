How a point is earned

You send in work. A computer checks it. If it passes, we give it a stamp. The stamp is your point. If you said you want to be shown, your name goes here.You send work, a machine checks it, and we stamp what passes. A point needs all four: you said yes to being shown, the stamp is ours and in our public log, the stamp matches the exact file, and a proof has no unfinished steps. Nothing else adds points.A row needs: the handle opted in on the Founders form; sha256(file) equals stamp.hash; verifyStamp(stamp, key, log) is VALID; for Lean, no sorry/admit/native_decide outside comments (the full lake build plus axioms check ran before signing). A stamp counts once. Source: labor_board.mjs; done-check: landing-tests/labor_test.mjs.
