How it works

Three steps, every time.

01

Paste it

Any answer, post or homework. We split it into the claims inside it.

The four labels

How sure we are, in one word.

Proven

Checked by a computer proof. It holds, every time.

Measured

Matched to a real measurement and its source.

Reasoned

A careful argument, each link shown. Not a proof.

Guess

Nothing backs it yet. We say so out loud.

    Questions

    What people ask first.

    Who does the checking?

    A machine. Math goes through a proof checker (Lean 4); numbers are matched to their source; arguments are traced link by link. People write the rules, not the verdicts.

    What if you are wrong?

    Every receipt can be rerun by anyone. If a label breaks, the receipt shows where, and the label changes in public.

    Why not just say true or false?

    Because most claims are not proven either way. The label says how sure anyone can honestly be.

    Is my text kept?

    Only what you choose to stamp. A stamp keeps a fingerprint of the file, not the file.
