II.Pitch in

Want to help? Tell us one thing you are good at and one thing you want help with. Then you get one small job to try. [RULE]

A seat is earned by doing the work, not by talking about it. A grown-up should fill this in with you. [RULE]

If your strength is my weakness, we need you. People who can check AI code are wanted most, plus mathematicians, explainers and video makers. Remote, any time zone. [GOAL: who we hope finds this]

The work is the application. A seat is earned by one logged contribution: a proof a machine accepts, a checker that catches mistakes planted on purpose, or an AI mistake you caught that holds up when checked again. [RULE]

Wanted most: people who review AI-written code. Seats go to the first accepted contributions: a Lean proof that compiles with 0 sorry and standard axioms, a checker that catches planted faults, or a reproducible AI error report. [RULE]

This form stores one application in Cloudflare KV through a Durable Object writer, rate limited per connection, deduplicated by Idempotency-Key and by GitHub name per day. [MEASURED: functions/api/founders.js]

Not promised: no reply time, no title, no pay and no equity. Daniel reads applications himself and picks seats by proven work. Any future reward rules are not set and need a lawyer's check first. [RULE]

Join the Founders Circle

Four short answers and a link. You get one starter task as your trial. No password, nothing to install. [MEASURED: the form below]

Open it as a GitHub issue · How to send it in

What is stored: your five answers, the trial task and the day, in Cloudflare storage. Daniel reads them. Only if you tick the box are your GitHub name, strength and weakness shown publicly. Not stored: your email, your address (IP) or a cookie. For the limit of 3 a day, a one-way code made from your connection and a key that changes daily is kept 2 days. To delete yours, email hello@humanitieshelpinghands.com with your GitHub name. Privacy [MEASURED: functions/api/founders.js]
