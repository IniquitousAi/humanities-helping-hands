What the labels mean

PROVEN means a computer checked it, step by step. Each fact says how, and what it does not cover.

MEASURED and GUESS are not proofs. Each one says why it has its label. A label can be wrong. Tell us and we will fix it.

PROVEN means a machine checked it. Most facts in the library are Lean proofs; the rest have only their numbers checked by Lean, so their real-world part stays OPEN. Each one says what it does not cover. A claim you type into the checker gets an exact computer check of its arithmetic instead, which proves the numbers, never the real-world reading of them.

MEASURED and GUESS are not proofs, and each one shows its reason. Labels can be wrong or change when new evidence arrives; tell us and we will fix it.

PROVEN means machine-checked. Every PROVEN fact in the library is a Lean 4 theorem accepted by the kernel (no sorry, standard axioms only); an ARITHMETIC-CHECKED fact has only its arithmetic proved in Lean, and its real-world reading stays OPEN. Each names its scope. A claim you type into the checker is decided by exact arithmetic (BigInt, no floating point), which proves the arithmetic, never the modeling claim that maps the numbers to the world.

MEASURED and GUESS carry no proof; each shows its evidence. Labels are revisable: a failed re-check or new evidence lowers or raises them, and a report from you triggers a re-check.

Paying

Reading and using HHH is open to everyone. You pay only if you want us to check your own file: $1 for one stamp, $10 a month for 100 checks, or $99 a month for a company.

If your file does not pass the check, you get no stamp and all your money back. You can stop a monthly plan any time by writing to hello@humanitieshelpinghands.com.

One Stamp is $1: we check one file, and only if it passes with no gaps (no sorry) do we sign a stamp for it. Checker is $10 a month for 100 checks. Proven Standing is $99 a month for a company's public claims.

A file that fails the check gets no stamp and a full refund. Monthly plans renew each month until you cancel; a canceled plan, or one whose renewal fails, runs to the end of the month already paid.
