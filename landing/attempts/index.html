I.How to read a row

CHECKED

Where it broke was read in a named source. A source is someone else's word.

REASONED

A likely break step with its reason. It is not read in a source yet.

NOT TRACED

Not accepted yet. The break step is not logged yet. That is open work.

II.The attempts

Many people tried. They wrote papers. For some of them, a math mistake was found. We log all of them. No one repeats an old mistake. Each row has a tag. Blue means a source says where it broke. Orange is our best reason. Gray is open work.

I.How to read a row

CHECKED

Where it broke was read in the named source. A source, not a machine proof.

REASONED

A likely break step, with its reason laid out, not yet read in a source.

NOT TRACED

Unaccepted, but where it broke is not logged yet. Pick one: that is open work.

I.Taxonomy and Status Nomenclature

CHECKED

Flaw locus confirmed by explicit bibliographic citation or peer-reviewed counterexample analysis.

REASONED

Hypothesized failure point supported by published barrier obstruction arguments, with its deductive rationale stated.

NOT TRACED

Unverified submission lacking formalized structural error localization. Open triage target.
