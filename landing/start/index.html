II.Each weakness, and its counter

People and computers are good at different things. Together, each helps the other.

Humans and machines fail in complementary ways. Paired together, each cognitive system compensates for the specific limitations of the other.

Complementary failure modes: human cognitive heuristics exhibit bounded working memory; automated verification provides deterministic soundness.
