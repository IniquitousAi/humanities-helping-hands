The Proof Tower

Every fact stands on
the floor below it.

All knowledge on HHH is sorted on one tower of nine fixed floors, logic at the bottom. The rule: a fact on a floor may rest only on the floors below it.

Nine floors

From the top down.

  1. Meaning and value
  2. Minds and people
  3. Measurement
  4. Statistics
  5. Probability
  6. Computation
  7. Arithmetic and math
  8. Sets and types
  9. Logic

Floor 0 is logic. The lower floors can be proven outright; the higher a floor is, the more of its facts are measured, reasoned or guessed, and their labels say so.

The labels

Four labels, the same on every floor.

PROVEN

Only for a claim a machine checked.

MEASURED

Tested or measured, and the page says which test.

REASONED

Backed by sources or studies, with how strong the evidence is.

GUESS

Not checked yet. The page says which checker is missing.
