Questions

Asked most often.

Does a passing check mean my idea is true?

It means the machine accepted the statement exactly as written. The receipt says plainly what it did not check, like whether the formal statement matches what you meant.

Does my file get stored?

The pay page only ever carries the hash of your file. You email the file after paying, and the receipt records its hash so anyone can match it.

What if the check cannot run?

You get a full refund. A file that runs and fails still gets a receipt that says where it failed.

How do I cancel?

One email. The plan stops at the end of the month you paid for.
